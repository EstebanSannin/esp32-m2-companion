## What's already set

- 4 layers (SIG/GND/PWR/SIG per SPEC), 0.8 mm, outline+fingers from the
  spec-verified `M2_KeyB_2242_EdgeFingers` footprint (J1 at board origin).
- DRC: JLCPCB minima (0.127 mm track/clearance, 0.3 mm drill, 0.45 mm via);
  edge-clearance waived for J1 only (`.kicad_dru`); intra-footprint mask
  bridges allowed (0.5 mm finger pitch merges apertures — standard).
- Netclass `USB_DIFF` on all USB nets. **Width/gap are placeholders
  (0.15/0.15): run JLCPCB's impedance calculator for the 0.8 mm 4-layer
  stackup (JLC04081H) and set the real 90 Ω geometry before routing.**
- Antenna keep-out rule area (all copper layers), y −37.6..−31.4 card coords.

## Placement rationale (adjust freely)

- Module top edge 4.5 mm below card top: the M.2 screw head owns the
  top-center (Ø~6 zone; the J1 footprint carries its own keep-out there).
- USBLC6 (U1) near fingers 7/9; diff pair runs up the right side to module
  pins 13/14 through bottom-side R7/R8 (0R series, HDG provision) — route
  edge→U1→(via)→R7/R8→module, referenced to L2 GND, no stubs.
- Bottom side (faces the CARRIER underside at ~2.5 mm in the Mallow slot —
  the card top faces away from the host PCB, i.e. downward on Mallow):
  test points TP1–TP6, USB series/shunt provision, module 3V3 decoupling
  (C6/C7) + EN RC (C5/R1) under module pins 1–3.
- Power entry FB1 near VCC fingers (left); bulk row behind it.

## Known-open items

1. ~~J3 header~~ **Resolved (ADR 0005)**: JST SH-4 Qwiic (J3) + SH-8 (J4),
   assembled, top side. Their courtyards consume the whole lower top zone,
   which forced item 2:
2. **Top/bottom split is now: top = module + connectors; bottom = all
   passives + USBLC6 + diodes + LEDs + TPs.** The owner's "no bottom
   components" wish is geometrically incompatible with two top-side
   connectors — the two constraints cannot both hold on 22 mm width.
   Consequence: two-sided assembly (or ~15 min hand-soldering) — GATE 4.
   LEDs are SIDE-VIEW parts at the left card edge (ADR 0003 amendment):
   they emit out of the slot gap and are visible in any orientation.
3. Residual DRC: ~12 courtyard-overlap infos (sub-0.4 mm kisses, fine-tune
   while routing) + 3 J1-internal artifacts (footprint's own mounting pad
   inside its own screw keep-out — benign). No shorts, no clearance errors.
4. Bevel/panel decision (risks R2): JLCPCB bevel is 30° and needs ≥50×50 mm
   panels with fingers on the panel edge — or skip bevel for v1.
5. USB 90 Ω geometry: run JLCPCB's impedance calculator for the 0.8 mm
   4-layer stackup and update the USB_DIFF netclass before routing.
