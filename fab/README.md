Generated by `make fab` from the routed board. Upload these 3 files to
JLCPCB's [instant quote](https://cart.jlcpcb.com/quote):

| File | JLCPCB slot |
|---|---|
| `esp32_m2_companion_gerbers.zip` | **Gerber file** (PCB tab): 6 copper layers + masks, silk, paste, outline; drill as separate PTH / NPTH files. No `.gbrjob`: its stackup / finish fields are KiCad defaults that contradict the order form (RC1 delta review dfm-r4); JLC orders from the web form |

Also generated, **not uploaded**: `polarity_checklist.md`. It says what JLC's placement preview must
show for every polarized part (step 3 below).

`make fab` exits **2 = ORDER BLOCKED** until every polarized part's rotation in
`hardware/tools/gen_fab.py` `JLC_ROT` carries a date, meaning it was verified against JLC's preview
**for this revision**. The files are still written so they can be uploaded for that preview.
`make fab` also asserts that the gerber zip holds every copper layer of the board (6).

## Order settings (v2.0 Rev A; quote UI checked 2026-10-10)
- **Layers 6**, thickness **0.8 mm**.
- **Specify Stackup: Yes → JLC06081H-2116.** F / In1 GND / In2 / In3 / In4 +3V3 / B
  (ADR 0008, `docs/stackup.md`).
- **Min via hole size/diameter: 0.2mm/(0.3/0.35mm).** Required: 92 of the 255 vias are 0.35/0.2
  (owner-approved at GATE V3; the others: 50 × 0.45/0.2, 113 × 0.45/0.3; RC2 count). JLC adds two things automatically with this option, both expected:
  - **4-Wire Kelvin Test: Yes**
  - **Material: FR4 TG155**
- **Via Covering: Epoxy Filled & Capped.** This is the 6-layer default; the board relies on it for
  via-in-pad (module USB pads, escape vias).
- **Impedance Control: No requirement.** USB is Full-Speed (12 Mbps) only; the stackup is already
  specified.
- Surface finish **ENIG** (6 layers is ENIG-only). **Gold Fingers: Yes.**
- **Solder mask: green**, silkscreen white. Green keeps JLC's 0.1 mm mask dams; U3–U5 (0.4 mm
  pitch) and U6 have exactly 0.1 mm dams, and other colours need wider ones (RC1 review dfm-3).
  In the production-file check, confirm the dams between U3–U6 pads survived in JLC's B.Mask.
- **Beveling: No** (forced: JLC can't bevel boards under 50 × 50 mm). Hand-chamfer the leading edge
  lightly before first insertion: ~45°, 0.2–0.3 mm, both faces, leading corner only.
- **Castellated holes: No.** The mounting notch is routed in the outline (no plated edge feature). If
  JLC's DFM flags castellation, something is wrong: stop and check.
- Confirm Production File: **Yes**. Deburring/Edge rounding: Yes. Mark: Remove.
- **Mark on PCB → 2D Barcode (Serial Number): Number only, Specify Position: Yes.** The board
  carries the required solid 2 × 10 mm silkscreen rectangle on the back, under "samnium.tech"
  (`tools/v2_brand.py`). JLC prints prefix + incrementing number inside it, one row; fewer
  characters = larger font. Recommended: **prefix `V2A`, Unique Number: Remove, starting value
  `0001`** (prints `V2A0001`, `V2A0002`, ...). JLC does not guarantee a continuous sequence
  (extra or scrapped panels), and on a reorder **set the starting value by hand** (it does not
  reliably continue). JLC shows the number on a separate `qrb` layer in the production files.
  The board serial is for people; the unit identity stays the module MAC (Data Matrix): record
  serial ↔ MAC at bring-up.
- **Assembly: both sides** (top: U2, J3, J4, R33; bottom: everything else).
- **13 Extended BOM lines** (feeder fee each): U2, U1, D1/D2, D3, D4, J3, J4, Q1–Q3, R31, R32,
  RN1–RN3, U3–U5, U6. No DNP parts in v2.

Cost reference (PCB only, quote 2026-10-10, settings above): 5 pcs $85.39 · 100 pcs $207.49.
Parts + assembly: see `docs/v2-gate-v4.md`.

## Order steps (from the Rev B order, GATE B3, 2026-10-13)
1. `make fab` → upload the 3 files above. Select **Confirm Parts Placement: Yes** and **Confirm
   Production File: Yes**. The real checks happen after ordering, and production waits for your
   confirmation.
2. The placement preview shown **before** ordering is generated automatically ("for reference
   only"): JLC's 3D models may sit off their pads and polarity marks can mislead. Note anything odd,
   but **do not rotate parts in JLC's editor**. The CPL is the source of truth.
   **Order remark** (paste into the order notes):
   > Bottom side: U1, U3, U4, U5, U6 - pin 1 per the silkscreen dot/triangle. Their land patterns
   > are 180-degree symmetric; please confirm pin 1 against our silkscreen, do not rely on the CPL
   > rotation alone. Q1, Q2, Q3: pin 1 per the silkscreen triangle (180 degrees is electrically
   > equivalent for these; the pads must land). D3/D4 side-view LEDs: lens facing the nearest long
   > side edge, away from R3/R4 (not toward the gold fingers); "+" (anode) on the pad toward the gold fingers - the one wired to R3 pad 2 / R4 pad 2; cathode mark toward the screw notch.
   > Serial number: the solid 2x10 mm silkscreen rectangle on the bottom side (under
   > "samnium.tech") is only the position marker for the serial number; please replace it with
   > the number, do not print the solid block.
3. **Production files** (email, ~48 h to confirm):
   - Download JLC's zip and run `make jlc-check JLC=<zip>`. It must report **PASS** (stored upload
     identical, every drill hole matched).
   - Look at `hardware/build/jlc_check/diff_*.png`. Expected differences are listed in
     `tools/check_jlc_production.py`.
   - v2: check that all **6** copper layers are there.
   - **Serial field:** the number must be on the `qrb` layer inside the 2 × 10 mm field, and the
     solid white block must be **gone** from the back silkscreen (`bo`) - it is only a position
     marker. If the block is still there, ask JLC to remove it before confirming.
   - Check the panel: V-cut on the long sides only, nothing on the finger edge, no castellation.
4. **Final DFM placement** (engineers, "Confirm" button on the order page): check every row of
   `fab/polarity_checklist.md` (18 parts) using N/S: N = screw-notch edge, S = gold fingers.
   - **LEDs D3/D4: check BOTH.** The "+" (anode) must be on the pad toward the gold fingers, the one wired to R3 pad 2 / R4 pad 2,
     **and** the lens must face the nearest long side edge, away from R3/R4 (not the gold fingers;
     "left" in the top view, mirrored in JLC's bottom preview). v1 got the lens right but polarity
     wrong; Rev B got polarity right but the lens inward.
   - If in doubt, ask the engineers to confirm against the physical part on the tape.
   - New in v2: U3–U6, Q1–Q3, RN1–RN3; U1's offset changed (0 → 270). Offsets follow the
     bottom-side rule in `gen_fab.py` (library rotation + 180°, RC1 review dfm-1): CPL U3 0,
     U4/U5 90, Q1–Q3 90, U6 0, U1 270. Their lands are 180°-symmetric, so a wrong offset seats
     perfectly and swaps pins (U3–U5 GND↔VDD, U6 IN↔OUT, U1 GND↔VBUS = dead card): check pin 1
     (silk dot/triangle) of **every** one of U1, U3–U6, by N/S.
   - Q1–Q3: check pin 1 too (silk triangle), but a 180° turn is electrically equivalent
     (cross-drained SOT-563): don't hold the order on it. Only a 90° error matters (pads would not
     land). RN1–RN3: only the 90° matters.
   - **Never approve a placement by eye.** If something is wrong, reply to the engineers on the
     order page.
5. After confirming, record the confirmed rotations with today's date in `JLC_ROT`
   (`hardware/tools/gen_fab.py`) so the next `make fab` exits 0.

Do not order before GATE V4 is approved by the owner. See `docs/v2-gate-v4.md`.
